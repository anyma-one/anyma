# The 64 hexagrams

The Chinese word for these figures is *gua* (卦), a divination sign laid out to be read. It covers both the six-line hexagrams and the three-line trigrams. In English, "hexagram" (Greek for "six marks") is the usual term, and it's the one this app uses.

Each hexagram is listed with its traditional number, its glyph, the name used in this app (Alfred Huang's), its pinyin and Chinese character, Richard Wilhelm's name for reference, and its two trigrams, upper over lower.
