What is the I Ching?

One of The Oldest Books and Divination Methods in The World

Its Chinese name, Yìjīng 易經, means "the Classic of Changes." At its core are 64 figures of six lines, each with a short text, compiled in China close to three thousand years ago. For most of its life it has been two things at once: a way to consult on decisions that mattered, and a book of philosophy about how change moves through nature and through a life.
