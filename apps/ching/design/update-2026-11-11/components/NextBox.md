# NextBox and MakeItYours (new)

The margin box that leads on from the current level.

- Surface `--surface-ember` with border and highlight; padding 22px desktop margin, clamp(24px, 3.4vw, 34px) elsewhere.
- Eyebrow bronze: "Next · Deep Cast" / "Next · Full Cast"; on Full Cast "Full Cast".
- Heading Young Serif 24px (margin) or 28px: the next level's description, or "Make It Yours" on Full Cast.
- Button: important. Labels: "deep cast →", "full cast →", "begin level III →" (Roman numeral kept in capitals).
