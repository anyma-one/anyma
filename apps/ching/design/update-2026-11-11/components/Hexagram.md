# Hexagram (changed)

The six-line figure, drawn from bars, bottom line first.

- Line: two bars. Yang: no gap (draw as one solid bar or remove the seam). Yin: gap 16 to 18px on the toss and reading figures; smaller figures scale the gap with the bar.
- Empty slot (not yet cast): ONE continuous 1px outline in `--line-empty`, exactly the size of a drawn line, no centre divider.
- Changing line: cinnabar `#991613`, mark ○ (old yang, 9) or × (old yin, 6) to its right, number of the total to its left.
- On lacquer lines are bronze; on paper they are ink `#191411`.
- Sizes: toss 150 to 220px wide × 16px; reading desktop 150 to 200 × 15px; tablet header 160 × 12px with 2px between rows; mobile 210 × 15px.
- Reading layout: the figure sits beside Traditional Meaning; the upper and lower trigram pair (84px bars, 9px thick, 9px apart) sits beside Image.
