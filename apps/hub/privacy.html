Data & Privacy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

The controller for data processing on anyma is Wojtek Plichta, Tiefenbruchstraße 43a, 44651 Herne, Germany. Contact: hello@anyma.one. Our imprint has the full details.

This policy covers the anyma start screen — the page listing the projects. Each project processes its own data and has its own policy, linked from within it. If you have not opened a project, only this page applies to you.

1. Visiting this page

Simply opening the start screen creates no account, sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser. Our host records standard server logs, which may include technical data such as your IP address, for security and to deliver the page. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f)).

2. The suggestion form

If you send a project suggestion, we process the title and the text you write. You may also give an email address — this is optional, and we use it for one purpose only: to reply to your suggestion.

  • Legal basis: your consent, given by sending the form (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by writing to hello@anyma.one, without affecting earlier processing.
  • The suggestion is stored with our database provider, Supabase (in the EU), and delivered to our inbox through our email provider, Resend.
  • We delete your email address once the exchange is finished. The suggestion itself may be kept without it.
  • If you leave the email field empty, the suggestion contains no personal identifier at all. Please do not write personal details into the free-text field.

3. Cookies and local storage

This page uses no cookies of any kind — none for tracking, none for advertising, and none that are strictly necessary — and writes nothing to your browser's local storage. There is therefore no cookie banner. If we ever add non-essential cookies or third-party tracking, we will add a consent banner and ask first.

4. Analytics

There is no analytics or tracking of any kind on this page. We do not measure visits to the start screen and do not build a profile of you.

5. Hosting and international transfers

The site is hosted by Vercel. Our database provider, Supabase, stores data in the EU, so no transfer outside the EEA is involved there. Two providers are located in or transfer data to the United States: Vercel (hosting and server logs, which may include technical data such as IP addresses) and Resend (which delivers suggestion emails).

Where data is transferred to the United States, it is safeguarded by appropriate measures under Chapter V of the GDPR, in particular the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, as set out in each provider's Data Processing Agreement, which we have accepted. You can request details at hello@anyma.one.

6. How long we keep data

  • An email address given with a suggestion is kept only until the exchange about that suggestion is over, then deleted.
  • The suggestion text itself is kept so we can consider it when planning future projects.
  • Server logs are kept for the period set by our host.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and objection, and where processing rests on consent, to withdraw it at any time. To exercise any of these, contact hello@anyma.one.

You may also complain to a supervisory authority. For North Rhine-Westphalia this is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW).

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the version published here, with the date above, is the current one.
